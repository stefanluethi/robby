# Trace link benchmark, 2026-10-26

Hardware campaign of [the plan](../plan/protobuf-tracing.md) §7: the sonde firmware
(baseline) and the protobuf firmware (nanopb on the STM32, robby outpost on the ESP32),
measured on robby with the tools in `tools/bench`.

## Summary

- **The protobuf link carries all IMU samples with room to spare.** All 806 samples/s
  arrive with no gaps, against 403/s (decimated) with sonde. It uses **8 % of the UART**
  instead of 69 %, because each sample costs **~7 B on the UART instead of 159 B**
  (10 B/sample when the other traces are counted in).
- **sonde can't carry all samples.** With decimation off it fills the 1 Mbaud link
  (83 kB/s), delivers only 521 of 806 samples/s with 176 gaps (up to 141 ms) in 60 s,
  and the distance, battery and task traces stop entirely.
- **The ESP32 is now the bottleneck, not the UART.** The whole chain carries
  **~5.8k samples/s cleanly (~440 kB/s on WiFi)** and saturates at **~7k samples/s
  (~530 kB/s)**: the ESP32 CPU is at 96 % (idle 3.6 %) and its queue starts dropping.
  At that point the UART is only at 60 %; it would saturate at ~11–12k samples/s
  (85 % load, then the STM32 drops records).
- **The plain WiFi limit of the ESP32-C6** (outpost generating the data itself, no UART):
  clean up to **8k samples/s / 607 kB/s**, first drops at 9k / 683 kB/s, peak ~700 kB/s
  (≈ 5.6 Mbit/s), at RSSI −61 to −64 dBm.
- **Headroom:** the robot's stream today (806 samples/s) uses ~1/7 of the chain's clean
  capacity. Compared with sonde's maximum (521 samples/s), the chain carries **~11× more
  samples**.

How to read it (plan §5): the ESP32 runs out of CPU at ~7k samples/s (6.2k synthetic +
0.8k IMU) and drops frames from its UART→WebSocket queue, while the UART still has 40 %
left. Above that, the WiFi throughput even goes down a little (535 → 491 kB/s): the CPU
spends more time on frames it then drops. The UART itself tops out at ~85 % load
(11k requested), where the STM32 starts dropping records.

The plain WiFi/TCP path of the ESP32-C6 (esp-radio + embassy-net, 16 KiB TCP buffer,
4 KiB WebSocket frames) peaks at ~700 kB/s. The chain stops ~25 % earlier (530 kB/s),
because the ESP32 also receives, checks and decodes the UART. Note: in `bench-wifi` mode
the generator itself uses the CPU, so its `cpu_idle` isn't comparable with the chain's.

## Problems found

1. **`bench/load` dropped 87 % of its batches at 1000 samples/s** (the first ramp
   stopped at its first step). The backlog was measured from a batch's first sample,
   not from its last. Fixed during the campaign in A4's `51ad8f9`, with a host test.
2. **WiFi stalls of 200–350 ms overflow the ESP32 queue even at moderate load.** In a
   3k-samples/s step the WebSocket send stalled several times for up to 350 ms, and
   59 frames were dropped from the 32-frame queue (the next run of the same step was
   clean). At normal load (H1-a) the longest send was 0.6 ms and nothing was dropped.
3. **Sporadic ESP32 UART RX errors under load.** 1–6 CRC errors / RX errors per 20 s
   step from 3k samples/s on, none at normal load. This fits RX FIFO overruns while the
   CPU is busy with WiFi (risk noted in plan §8).
4. **An action answer can get lost under overload.** At 13k samples/s the final
   `bench/load {rate: 0}` got "no answer from the STM32": the `ActionResult` travels in
   the same uplink queue that was dropping frames. The next request worked.

## Not measured

- **H1-c, baud sweep (2–4 Mbaud):** skipped. At 1 Mbaud the UART still has 40 % left
  when the ESP32 saturates, so a faster UART can't raise the limit.
- **H1-w close to the AP:** needs the robot moved; all runs were at its normal place
  (RSSI −58 to −64 dBm).
- Each ramp step ran once; WiFi stalls (problem 2) make single steps near the limit
  vary from run to run.

## Next steps (suggestions)

- To raise the limit, make the ESP32 cheaper per sample: profile the translation /
  CBOR encoding on the target, send fewer, larger WebSocket frames, and try turning off
  WiFi power saving (untested).
- Robustness: a larger or byte-based uplink queue against WiFi stalls (problem 2), UART
  RX by DMA (UHCI) on the ESP32 (problem 3), and send `ActionResult`s ahead of the
  traces (problem 4).
